## Added

* Setting to choose the maximum number of search results (100, 200, 500, 1000 or 2000).
* Drive connection status tracking for external removable drives

## Improved

* Configured folders on removable drives are now preserved while disconnected
* Photos from disconnected drives are now excluded from search
* Parallel OCR scan processing for up to 2x faster text indexing
* Limited search control width for a more consistent layout

## Fixed

* Photo-viewer keyboard shortcuts now work even when their associated controls are off-screen
