## Features

* Choose how your gallery is displayed: Justified, Square or classic Waterfall (Mansory) layouts
* New page headers make folders, searches and similar-image results easier to identify
* Improved thumbnail presentation and image sizing across gallery layouts
* Database maintenance now cleans up unused search-index data automatically

## Fixes
* Fixed bug with new photos appeared at search pages
