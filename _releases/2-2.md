## Features

* Zoom with a mouse, touchscreen, or touchpad in the photo detail view
* Expanded "Recent" section with more photos
* Larger search panel
* Fit to screen in the detail view
* View photos at their original size
* Redesigned status bar in the detail view
* You can see up to 200 000 photos from a single folder

## Fixes

* Fixed the search bar positioning on high-density (retina) displays
